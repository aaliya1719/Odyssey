# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Odyssey to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Focus on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give and receive actionable feedback.
- Protect private information shared in the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the project maintainer through the private contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
